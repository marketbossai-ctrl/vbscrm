What's Missing / Broken

❌ No Title Tags
Every page is missing a <title> tag. This is the single most important on-page SEO element. Google has nothing to display in search results.
❌ No Meta Descriptions
Every page is missing a meta description. No control over what Google shows in the snippet below the title.
❌ No H1 or H2 Headings
No heading hierarchy on any page. Google has no way to understand what each page is about.
❌ No Structured Data
No JSON-LD, no LocalBusiness schema, no FAQ schema, no Product schema. Google cannot display rich results (star ratings, phone, hours) in search.
❌ 222 Characters of Text
Homepage has essentially no content. Googlebot can render JS but there's nothing to rank.
❌ JS-Rendered Content
All content depends on Duda's JavaScript loader. SEO tools (Screaming Frog, Ahrefs) see blank pages. Crawl budget is wasted.
❌ No Blog or Content Strategy
Zero blog posts, zero articles. Site is a static brochure with no way to attract organic traffic.
❌ Only 6 Pages
Tiny site footprint. Competitors with 20+ pages of service content, location pages, and blog posts dominate.
❌ No Location Pages
Serves all of Hampton Roads but has zero city-specific pages. No "Virginia Beach glass contractor" page, no "Norfolk shower doors" page.
❌ Stale Content
Contact page untouched since August 2022 (4 years). Homepage last updated March 2025.
❌ No Alt Text on Images
Massive missed opportunity for image search traffic. People search for shower door designs and glass etching examples.
❌ No Social Proof
Google reviews not found in search results. No embedded review widget. No testimonial schema.

What Works

✓ SSL — Valid HTTPS with HSTS preload
✓ Security — X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, CSP headers present
✓ Sitemap — sitemap.xml exists and is properly formatted

✓ Canonical — Proper canonical URL on homepage

✓ Contact Info — Phone, email, address, hours all present
✓ Social — Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, Google Maps links found
✓ NAP Consistency — Name, address, phone consistent across pages

The Fix List (Priority Order)

1Title Tags — Immediate
Every page needs a unique, keyword-rich title tag (55–65 chars). Example: "Shower Doors & Custom Glass Etching | Brilliant Glass | Virginia Beach, VA"
2Meta Descriptions — Immediate
Every page needs a compelling meta description (150–160 chars) with a call to action. "Virginia Beach's trusted glass contractor. Custom shower enclosures, office partitions, mirrors, and glass etching. Free quotes. Call (757) 271-3060."
3H1 + Heading Structure — Immediate
Every page needs exactly one H1. Sub-sections need H2s. Homepage H1: "Custom Shower Doors & Glass Etching in Virginia Beach"
4LocalBusiness Schema — Immediate
Add JSON-LD with business name, address, phone, hours, service area, aggregate rating, and social profile links.
5Service Pages — This Week
Break "Services" into individual pages: shower enclosures, custom glass/mirrors, glass etching, office partitions. Each with 300+ words, unique title, schema, and before/after photos.
6Location Pages — This Week
Create pages for each city: glass contractor Virginia Beach, shower doors Norfolk, glass installation Chesapeake. Each with unique content, local schema, and embedded Google Map.
7Review Strategy — This Week
Claim and optimize GBP. Embed Google reviews on the website. Add Review schema markup. Generate 2–3 new reviews per month.
8Content Strategy — Ongoing
Blog posts: "How to choose the right shower door," "Glass etching vs. frosted film," "5 signs your shower door needs replacement," "Commercial glass partitions: privacy vs. open plan." 1–2 posts per month.
9Image SEO — Ongoing
Add descriptive alt text to every portfolio image. Compress for faster load times. Use descriptive filenames.
10Portfolio Expansion — Ongoing
Add captions with project descriptions and locations. Include before/after galleries.
