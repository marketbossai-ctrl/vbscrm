Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our glass services in Hampton Roads

How much does a frameless shower door cost in Virginia Beach?

Frameless shower doors in Hampton Roads typically range from $800 to $2,500 depending on size, glass thickness, and hardware. Custom enclosures with multiple panels or specialty glass can range higher. Contact us for a free, no-obligation quote tailored to your exact bathroom dimensions.

What areas do you serve?

Brilliant Glass serves all of Hampton Roads including Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, and Hampton. We install shower doors, office partitions, custom mirrors, and glass etching at homes and businesses throughout the region.

How long does glass installation take?

Most shower door installations take 1–2 hours once the glass is fabricated. Custom orders typically require 5–10 business days for fabrication, then installation is scheduled at your convenience. Commercial projects vary by scope — we will give you a timeline during your free consultation.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation quotes for all glass projects in Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, and Hampton. Simply contact us by phone or fill out our online form, and we will schedule a time to assess your space and provide a detailed estimate.

What types of glass do you work with?

We work with clear glass, frosted glass, low-iron glass, tinted glass, patterned glass, and tempered safety glass. We also offer glass etching for decorative and privacy applications. Every project uses tempered safety glass where required by building code.
