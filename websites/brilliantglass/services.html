Our Glass Services

From residential shower doors to commercial glass partitions, Brilliant Glass provides professional glass solutions for every need across Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, and Hampton.

Shower Enclosures

Custom frameless and framed shower doors designed and installed for your exact bathroom dimensions. Whether you are remodeling or building new, a professionally installed glass shower enclosure transforms the look and feel of your bathroom. Every enclosure is custom measured, precision cut, and installed with meticulous attention to waterproofing and finish detail.

Areas served: Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, Hampton

Office Glass Partitions

Modern glass walls and room dividers for commercial spaces. Glass partitions create open, light filled work environments while providing acoustic separation and privacy where needed. We work with office managers, contractors, and business owners to design and install partitions that fit the space, the budget, and the workflow. From single office dividers to full floor layouts.

Areas served: Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, Hampton

Custom Glass and Mirrors

Bespoke mirrors, glass tabletops, custom shelves, and specialty glass cut to your specifications. Any size, any thickness, any shape — if you can describe it, we can create it. Our custom glass work appears in homes, gyms, retail stores, restaurants, and professional offices throughout Hampton Roads.

Areas served: Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, Hampton

Glass Etching

Decorative and privacy glass etching for doors, windows, shower enclosures, and partitions. Etching adds elegance, branding, or privacy to any glass surface without sacrificing light. We offer custom designs, frosted privacy patterns, and logo etching for commercial spaces. Each etching is done in-house with professional grade equipment and materials.

Areas served: Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, Hampton
