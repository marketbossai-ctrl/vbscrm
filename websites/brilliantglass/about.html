About Brilliant Glass

Brilliant Glass has been serving Hampton Roads with professional glass installation and custom craftsmanship from day one. Based in Virginia Beach, we take pride in every project — from frameless shower enclosures that transform a bathroom to commercial glass partitions that redefine an office space.

Our Commitment to Quality

Glass is serious business. Every piece we cut, every enclosure we install, and every mirror we hang reflects our commitment to precision, safety, and beauty. We do not cut corners. We do not rush. We treat every project as if it were going into our own home or office.

No job is too small for professional care. Whether you need a single custom mirror or an entire office suite of glass partitions, you get the same attention to detail and the same standard of workmanship.

Serving All of Hampton Roads

From the Oceanfront to downtown Norfolk, from Chesapeake to Portsmouth to Hampton, our team brings professional glass services to homes and businesses throughout the region. We know the area. We know the building styles. And we know what it takes to deliver glass work that lasts in a coastal environment.
